Warning Signs for HVAC Repair

Spotting the early warning signs gives you the chance to act before the situation spirals into something far more expensive. Keep an eye out for the following clues that it's time to bring in an expert:

  • Air at the registers that is the wrong temperature for the mode you selected
  • A breaker that trips whenever the system tries to run
  • The outdoor unit humming but refusing to actually start
  • Airflow at the registers that feels weak in every room
  • Grinding, squealing, buzzing, or banging you have not heard before
  • Puddles near the furnace or air handler from a blocked drain line
  • Frequent on-off cycling while the house never gets to the thermostat setting
  • Rising utility costs even though the house feels worse

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Repair

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Have the system professionally serviced twice a year, once before summer and once before winter
  • Replace the air filter every one to three months to keep airflow strong across the blower and coil
  • Call about unusual noises early, since a squealing bearing costs far less to fix before the motor seizes
  • Keep supply and return registers open and unblocked in every room
  • During heat waves and cold snaps, set the thermostat a few degrees closer to the outdoor temperature to ease the load
  • Run a vinegar flush through the condensate line each spring to prevent clogs and float switch trips
