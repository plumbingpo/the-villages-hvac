The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

Filters, a clear outdoor unit, open registers, and a quick look at the drain are the homeowner's share of maintenance, and they matter. Everything beyond that needs a meter. Failures that leave you without heat or air rarely look like anything: an 80 percent capacitor looks like a healthy one, and a low charge is visible only in superheat and subcooling. A crack in the heat exchanger, a lazy flame signal, a pressure switch near its limit, or an amp draw drifting toward the breaker rating all stay hidden until someone measures them, and each is less expensive to handle a season early.

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of vegetation and rinse the coil gently each spring
  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
  • Flush the condensate line at the start of cooling season before algae blooms in it
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • Schedule maintenance in early spring and early fall, ahead of the busy season and the extreme weather
  • Check carbon monoxide alarms monthly in any home with fuel-burning appliances
  • Change the filter every one to three months; it is the single highest-value habit there is
